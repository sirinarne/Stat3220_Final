## NBA Player Salary Analysis 

## Overview 

---

This project analyzes how various performance and demographic related factros influence NBA player salaries, providing insight into what drives variation in salaries. 


### Research Questions:

1. Do NBA players in the Western Conference earn higher average salaries compared to
players in the Eastern Conference?

2) Do NBA players who average more minutes of playing time per game in a season earn
higher salaries than players who average fewer minutes per game? 

3. Are NBA player salaries higher for the “old” age group compared to the other age
groups (prime, young, veteran)?


--- 

### Tools Used:

- R
- R Studio

---

### Data 

- Two primary sources were merged for this analysis. One consisted of player salaries from the 2024-2025 season and the other included player statistics from the 2023-2024 season. These data came from ESPN and NBA. 

[NBA Player Salaries] https://www.espn.com/nba/salaries/_/year/2025/seasontype/3

[Players Advanced Stats] https://www.nba.com/stats/players/advanced?sort=W&dir=-1&Season=2023-24&SeasonType=Regular+Season
